# QuantaEdge Class 6/7/8 content completeness policy

This is a non-negotiable product rule for every future content run.

## Zero-omission requirement
Use the selected current NCERT chapter inventory as the internal source of truth. Do not stop at matching the number or names of chapters. For every chapter, build a traceable map for all relevant source headings and subheadings, definitions, key terms, rules/formulae, worked examples, figures/diagrams, tables, activities/investigations, safety notes, recap points and end-of-chapter learning outcomes. Every applicable source item must map to a QuantaEdge explanation, example, visual/activity, or explicitly documented not-applicable decision. Unmapped applicable items block publication.

## Original QuantaEdge expression
Books and student UI are QuantaEdge-authored teaching content. Do not print publisher names, source labels, audit language, draft watermarks or editorial-review notices in learner-facing PDFs. Keep source edition and mapping metadata in the internal Admin workflow only. Do not reproduce textbook passages or question wording verbatim; author original explanations, examples and assessments.

## Teaching quality gate per micro-topic
Each micro-topic needs a clear concept explanation, why/how reasoning, a worked example with meaningful steps, a real-life or cross-topic connection where appropriate, a common-misconception guard, a concise recap and a QuantaEdge Advanced connection when pedagogically suitable. Science content needs fact checks, safe activities and properly typeset Devanagari visual labels. Mathematics content needs independently checked calculations and clear fraction/diagram rendering.

## PDF and website separation
- Chapter-wise concept PDFs: teaching explanations, examples, recap and QuantaEdge Advanced only. No practice questions, MCQs, tests, answer keys or solutions.
- Complete-book PDF: all teaching material plus the approved-in-scope question bank, practice/test sections and answer explanations.
- Website: micro-topic teaching and per-topic practice/question bank; the chapter test stays separate. Answers must remain hidden until the configured attempt/reveal flow.
- Keep the previously approved question count unless the product owner explicitly changes it; improving teaching content is not a reason to inflate the question bank.

## Completion status
A chapter is not complete merely because a PDF exists or a JSON bundle passes schema validation. Record at least: curriculum coverage mapping, content/answer QA, PDF-policy QA, CMS draft import verification and student preview verification. The Admin publication gate remains manual/permissioned. Repeat this standard across all 10 Class 6 Maths chapters and all 12 Class 6 Science chapters before proceeding to subsequent classes.
